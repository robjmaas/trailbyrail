How to use TrailbyRail

Find trails near home or reachable by public transport — no car needed

1
📍

Enter your location

Type your address, neighbourhood, or city. Use the 📡 button to detect your location automatically. This is the starting point for all searches.

2
🏠

Near home — local trails

The default mode. Set Walk to stop and Walk from stop to trail to control how far you're willing to walk. Keep the Train Journey slider at 0 for purely local results.

3
🚂

Train journey — go further

Slide Train Journey Distance to the right to find trails further away reachable by train. The further you slide, the wider the search radius around potential destinations. Results show the full journey chain: home → station → train → trailhead.

4
🗺️

Search by destination

Switch to By destination to search around a specific place — useful when you already know roughly where you want to go. Your home address is still used for journey planning links.

5
🥾

Trail cards & results

Each card shows the trail name, distance, type, and nearest public transport stop. Tap 📍 Open in Maps for navigation, or ⬇️ GPX to download the route to your device.

6
🔄

Loop routes

Click Generate loop on any trail card to get a circular walking or cycling route starting from the trailhead. The route prefers off-road paths and avoids busy roads where possible.

7
🔗

Share a trail

Hit Share on any card to copy a link. When your friend opens it, the search runs automatically and the page scrolls straight to that trail — no hunting through results.

8
💾

Save searches

Create a free account and use the 💾 Save button to bookmark your favourite search settings. Re-run them in one tap from the saved search chips below the form.

9
✅

Mark trails as done

Logged in? Click Mark done on any card to keep track of where you've been. Completed trails are shown with a green border and can be hidden with the Hide completed filter.
